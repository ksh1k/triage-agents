# MITRE ATT&CK® data notice

`enterprise-attack-v20.1.json` in this folder is a compact, derived subset of
the MITRE ATT&CK® Enterprise matrix, version 20.1. It has one record per
active technique (ID, name, tactics, platforms, shortened description, URL)
plus a map from revoked IDs to their replacements.
